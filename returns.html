TWISTMODE RETURNS & REFUNDS

Returns & Refunds

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Return Window

Eligible return requests must be submitted within 30 days of delivery. Products must be unused, clean, and in their original condition to be considered for a return.

2. Used Products

Because TwistMode is a personal-use hair styling tool, products that have been used may not be eligible for return for hygiene reasons. Please contact TwistMode before sending any product back.

3. Made-to-Order Colors

Made-to-order colors may be non-returnable because they are produced specifically for the selected order. Return eligibility for a made-to-order product will be confirmed by TwistMode before a return is authorized.

4. Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged or you receive the wrong product, contact us as soon as possible. Please include your order information and photographs of the issue when helpful so we can review the order and determine the appropriate resolution.

5. Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method after the returned product has been received and inspected. Shipping charges may be non-refundable except where required by law or when TwistMode made an error with the order.

6. Contact Before Returning

Please contact us before sending a return so we can provide the current return instructions. shoptwistmode@gmail.com .
