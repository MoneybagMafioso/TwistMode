TWISTMODE SHIPPING POLICY

Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Where We Ship

TwistMode currently ships within the United States.

2. Ready-to-Ship Orders

Ready-to-ship colors are prepared for packing as quickly as practical after an order is received. Handling time may vary depending on order volume and current production demand.

3. Made-to-Order Colors

Made-to-order colors require additional production time before they can be packed and shipped. Production estimates are estimates only and are not guaranteed delivery dates.

4. Shipping Charges

Any applicable shipping charges will be shown during checkout before you complete your purchase. Promotional free-shipping offers, when available, may have additional conditions or minimum purchase requirements.

5. Tracking and Delivery

When tracking is available, tracking information will be provided through the order or shipping confirmation process. Carrier delivery dates are estimates controlled by the shipping carrier and are not guaranteed by TwistMode.

6. Shipping Address

Customers are responsible for providing an accurate and complete shipping address. If you notice an address mistake, contact TwistMode as soon as possible before the order ships. We cannot guarantee that an address can be changed after an order has entered the shipping process.

7. Contact

For shipping questions, contact us at shoptwistmode@gmail.com .
