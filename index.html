GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TwistMode only for curly hair?

TwistMode is made for textured and curly hair with enough natural texture for the tool to grab and shape. It works best on medium to tight curls and textured hair, but it isn't designed for straight hair or very loose curls.

Which one should I get — Lite or XL?

Lite is best for shorter, coarse hair and tighter control. XL gives you more coverage and reach for short-to-long hair.

Can I wash TwistMode after using it?

Yes. TwistMode can be washed and reused. For a deeper clean, use a brush to remove trapped hair and product buildup, then rinse thoroughly and let it dry before using it again.

How do I know which size is right for me?

If the hair is shorter and you want more control, go Lite. For longer hair or more coverage, go XL.
